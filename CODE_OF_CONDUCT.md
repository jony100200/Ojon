# Community conduct

We welcome constructive contributions, reproducible reports and disagreement backed by evidence.

- Be professional and respectful to other contributors and users.
- Discuss code, methods and results rather than attacking people.
- Do not post harassment, threats or private personal information.
- Do not publish credentials, private prompts or confidential models in issues or pull requests.
- Do not fabricate benchmarks, test results or claims of model support.

Maintainers may edit or close discussions that undermine these expectations. If you need to report a serious issue privately, refer to [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
